Inter and Orbitron are hosted locally under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Their respective complete licenses are `inter-OFL.txt` and `orbitron-OFL.txt`.

The original variable TrueType files came from the official Google Fonts
repository. `provenance.json` records source URLs, original Git blob and SHA-256
hashes, the served WOFF2 hashes, and the conversion tool versions.

The WOFF2 files were compressed locally with fontTools 4.61.1 and Brotli 1.2.0,
without subsetting. Conversion preserved the complete character maps, variation
axes, glyph order, every glyph outline and the variable glyph data. Both fonts
include French accented characters and the Œ/œ ligatures. No font tooling is
required by the application or its build.
